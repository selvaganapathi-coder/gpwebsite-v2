📍 How to Reach

G.P. Pharmacy College is located on Vaniyambadi Main Road at Mandalavadi (Village & Post Office), Jolarpet – 635851, Tirupattur Taluk & District, Tamil Nadu.

  • By Train: Get down at Jolarpet Railway Junction (major halt on Chennai–Salem route). The campus is approximately 5 km from the station via Vaniyambadi Main Road.
  • By Bus: Buses ply between Jolarpet, Tirupattur, Vaniyambadi, and Ambur. Ask for Mandalavadi / GP Pharmacy College stop on Vaniyambadi Main Road.
  • By Car: Use Vaniyambadi Main Road from Jolarpet or Tirupattur — the college campus is on the left with GP Group signage.
