Our College Principal

Prof. Dr. S. Thenmozhi

M.Pharm., Ph.D — Principal, G.P. Pharmacy College

Prof. Dr. S. Thenmozhi leads G.P. Pharmacy College with a vision for excellence in pharmaceutical education, research, and student outcomes. Under her guidance, the institution maintains high academic standards, modern laboratory practices, and strong industry and hospital connections for student training and placements.

She oversees curriculum delivery across D.Pharm, B.Pharm, M.Pharm and Pharm-D programs, faculty development, research initiatives, and compliance with PCI, and university regulations. Her leadership emphasizes practical pharmacy training, ethical practice, and preparing graduates for diverse careers in healthcare and the pharmaceutical industry.

The Principal works closely with the Training & Placement Cell, department heads, and GP Group management to ensure every student receives mentorship, skill development, and opportunities for professional growth throughout their academic journey.
