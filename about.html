GP Group — Education for Every Community

Our Chairman Er. G. Ponnusamy, B.E., educationist is providing basic education to all middle class and poor peoples. He started ITI in 1979, then he started Tirupattur Arts and Science College, GP Polytechnic, GP College of Education and GP Pharmacy College.

GP Pharmacy College is the first Pharmacy college in Tirupattur as well as Vellore District too. It is started in 2019 with all the infrastructures required as per Pharmacy Council of India, New Delhi. Affiliated to The Tamil Nadu Dr. M.G.R. Medical University, we offer D.Pharm, B.Pharm, M.Pharm and Pharm-D with well-equipped laboratories, digital classrooms, AC library, computer labs, seminar halls, and separate hostel facilities for boys and girls.

We Are an Innovative Pharmacy Institution

G.P. Pharmacy College delivers industry-aligned pharmaceutical education with practical laboratory training, hospital exposure, and placement support. Students gain hands-on experience in pharmaceutics, pharmaceutical analysis, pharmacology, and clinical pharmacy through modern labs and smart classrooms.

The institution maintains strong ties with hospitals, pharma manufacturers, and retail chains for industrial visits, internships, and campus recruitment. Our faculty mentor students in research, publications, and professional development — preparing graduates for careers across hospital pharmacy, manufacturing, quality control, regulatory affairs, and clinical practice.
