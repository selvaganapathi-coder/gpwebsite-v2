The Management

Tirupattur and the Vellore region have long valued education as a path to opportunity. This vision was brought to life by Er. G. Ponnusamy, B.E., an educationist who has dedicated his life to providing quality education for middle-class and underserved communities since 1979.

Starting with an ITI in 1979, he expanded the GP Group to include Tirupattur Arts and Science College, GP Polytechnic, GP College of Education, and finally G.P. Pharmacy College in 2019 — the first pharmacy college in Tirupattur and the entire Vellore District. His mission has always been to remove barriers to education and create institutions where students can build meaningful careers.

Under his leadership, GP Group institutions focus on practical training, affordable access, and community service — ensuring that pharmacy and professional education reach students who need it most.
