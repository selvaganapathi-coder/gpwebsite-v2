About College

G.P. Pharmacy College, Tirupattur

PCI Approved Code 889 TN Dr. MGR University

Er. G. Ponnusamy's commitment to accessible education extends beyond mere provision — it's a passion to uplift communities through knowledge and skill development. Recognizing the need for vocational training, he established the ITI in 1979, then Tirupattur Arts and Science College, GP Polytechnic, GP College of Education, and G.P. Pharmacy College in 2019 — the first pharmacy college in Tirupattur and Vellore District.
